					Customer Service Performance & Call Centre Analytics

1. Project Overview
The project analyses customer service operations to understand call volumes, representative performance, time-based activity patterns and differences in operational performance across the company branches.

The dashboard is managing a combined view of customer service operations, helping monitor workload distribution, compare representative activity, spot operational patterns and support data-driven decision-making. The target was to reshape raw call-centre data into an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard employing Microsoft Power BI.

2. Business Requirements
The project was designed to tackle the following management questions:

1. How about this, what's probably the total call volume. As, well, how is it distributed between incoming and outgoing calls for each sales representative?
2. Which 10 sales representatives are, to be sure, handling the highest number of outgoing calls?
3. Which 10 representatives are handling the most calls, both incoming and outgoing, during the 2 PM time block?
4. As, well, how do the North and South branches compare for call volumes and call purposes?
5. What operational patterns can, of course, maybe be spotted from customer service data to support management decisions?

3. Dataset Description
The offered `CustomerService.xlsx` dataset is being employed by the assessment, covering 559 entries and 14 columns.
The dataset includes operational information such as:
- Representative ID
- Branch
- Call purpose
- Incoming or outgoing call category
- Time block
- Calls
- Incoming wait time
- Busy minutes
- Not-ready minutes
- Lost calls
- Abandoned calls

The dataset was reviewed and prepared in Power BI before formulating the needed calculations, filters and visualisations.

5. Work Performed
A. Data Preparation and Modelling
- Imported the Excel dataset into Power BI Desktop.
- Reviewed the available fields and their data types.
- Created DAX measures to find total call volumes and incoming/outgoing call distribution.
- Constructed extra measures and ranking logic to analyse representative performance.
- Employed visual-level filters to spot the top 10 representatives and separate the 2 PM time block.

B. Customer Service Overview
Devised an overview dashboard to monitor the overall performance of customer service operations.
The dashboard includes:
- Total calls handled.
- Lost and abandoned calls.
- Average incoming wait time.
- Busy and not-ready time.
- Incoming versus outgoing call distribution.
- Call volumes across the diverse time blocks.
- Call distribution by purpose.

C. Sales Representative and Calls Performance
Built a representative-level performance dashboard to analyse individual call-handling activity.
The assessment includes:
- Comparison of incoming and outgoing calls for each representative.
- Top 10 representatives by outgoing call volume.
- During the 2 PM time block, top 10 representatives by total call activity.
- Comparison of representative activity and call-handling patterns.

D. Company Sales Branches Comparison
Devised a branch-level comparison dashboard to gauge operational differences between the North and South branches.
The examination includes:
- Comparison of total call volumes between branches.
- Incoming and outgoing call distribution by branch.
- Comparison of call activity across the branches.
- Call-purpose comparisons to support operational planning.

6. Key Findings and Business Inferences
Based on the dashboard calculations and dataset investigation:
- 559 records analysed: The project scrutinized 559 customer service records.
- Incoming calls: 208 incoming calls were recorded, representing roughly 37.2% of the total.
- Outgoing calls: 351 outgoing calls were recorded, representing roughly 62.8% of the total.
- Call distribution: Outgoing calls accounted for the larger share of recorded call activity.
- Incoming wait time: The dashboard reported an average incoming wait time of about 3.02 minutes.
- Service quality monitoring: Lost calls and abandoned calls are covered as indicators that can help management study potential service-quality issues, on the whole.
- Branch-level differences: The branch comparison differs in call activity and call-purpose distribution between the North and South branches.
- Representative performance: Spot differences in call volumes and individual workload are being helped by representative-level analysis, in many respects.
- Time-based workload: A emphasized view of representative activity is being offered by the 2 PM analysis during that time block, in actual application.

7. Business Recommendations
The dashboard can support management in exploring the following operational advancements:

1. Workload Distribution

Across the representatives, review incoming and outgoing call allocation to spot workload imbalances and opportunities for better resource distribution.

2. Peak-Hour Staffing

Use the 2 PM time-block investigation to assess whether staffing levels and representative availability are suitable during the periods of higher call activity.

3. Service Efficiency

Monitor average incoming wait time, lost calls and dropped calls to spot potential service bottlenecks and study their fundamental causes.

4. Branch-Level Performance

Compare North and South branch call volumes and call purposes to spot differences in operational demand and raise resource planning, broadly speaking.

5. Representative Performance

Use representative-level call volumes as an operational monitoring measure, alongside service quality and other relevant performance indicators, in many respects.

These recommendations are intended to guide further investigation, and operational patterns are being spotted by the dashboard but doesn't independently establish the causes behind them, in actual application.
