# Power BI Portfolio — Business Intelligence & Data Analytics

This folder contains three Power BI projects demonstrating practical work in business intelligence, data visualisation, KPI development, operational analysis, and business problem-solving.

## Project 1 — Customer Service Performance & Call Centre Analytics

**Focus:** Customer Service Operations | Call Centre Analytics | Representative Performance

An interactive Power BI report analysing 559 customer-service records across call volumes, incoming and outgoing activity, representative workload, time blocks, and North/South branch performance.

**Dashboard coverage:**
- Customer service overview and call activity.
- Incoming versus outgoing calls.
- Top representative activity and peak 2 PM workload.
- North and South branch comparison.

## Project 2 — Is Least Profitable Always Unprofitable?

**Focus:** Retail Analytics | Profitability Analysis | Geographical Visualisation

Uses the Sample Superstore dataset to investigate product profitability across categories, individual products, US states, and quarterly periods from 2013 to 2016.

**Central business question:** Are the least profitable products consistently unprofitable, or does their profitability vary across geographical markets?

**Dashboard coverage:**
- Overall profit by category.
- Top 10 least profitable items.
- Profit by state.
- Quarterly profit trend (2013–2016).
- Department filter.

[Open project folder](Project_02_Supersore/) · [Power BI report](Project_02_Supersore/Least_Profitable_Products_Analysis.pbix) · [PDF report](Project_02_Supersore/Least_Profitable_Products_Analysis.pdf) · [Source workbook](Project_02_Supersore/Superstore.xlsx)

## Project 3 — Northstar Bank: Business Analysis & Transformation Dashboard

**Focus:** Business Analysis | UAT | Requirements Traceability | Defect Management | Service Operations

A two-page simulated banking case study combining requirements traceability, UAT execution, defect monitoring, service-request volumes, resolution time, and SLA classification.

**Page 1 — UAT Executive Dashboard**
- Requirements and test-case tracking.
- Test execution status and pass rate.
- Defect severity, status, and traceability.

**Page 2 — Service Request Operations**
- Total and resolved requests.
- SLA breach monitoring and average resolution time.
- Requests by category and resolution time by priority.
- SLA outcomes by assigned team.
- Category, Priority, and Branch filters.

**Simulation notice:** Northstar Bank and all associated requirements, test cases, defects, service records, and results are fictional and generated for portfolio demonstration. They do not represent real banking operations, real customer data, or production UAT.
