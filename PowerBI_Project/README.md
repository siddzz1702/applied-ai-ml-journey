# Power BI Portfolio — Business Intelligence & Data Analytics

This portfolio contains three Power BI projects developed to demonstrate practical skills in data analysis, business intelligence, data visualisation, dashboard development, and business problem-solving.

## Project 1 — Customer Service Performance & Call Centre Analytics

**Focus:** Customer Service Operations | Call Centre Analytics | Representative Performance

An interactive report analysing 559 customer-service records across call volumes, incoming and outgoing activity, representative workload, time blocks, and North/South branch performance.

**Dashboard coverage:** customer-service overview, representative-level performance, peak 2 PM activity, and branch comparison.

[Open project files](PROJECT_01_Customer_Service/) · [Power BI report](PROJECT_01_Customer_Service/Customer_Service_Analytics.pbix) · [PDF](PROJECT_01_Customer_Service/Customer_Service_Analytics.pdf)

## Project 2 — Is Least Profitable Always Unprofitable?

**Focus:** Retail Analytics | Profitability Analysis | Geographical Visualisation

Uses the Sample Superstore dataset to investigate product profitability across categories, individual products, US states, and quarterly periods from 2013 to 2016.

**Dashboard coverage:** profit by category, ten least profitable items, profit by state, quarterly profit trend, and department filtering.

[Open project files](Project_02_Supersore/) · [Power BI report](Project_02_Supersore/Least_Profitable_Products_Analysis.pbix) · [PDF](Project_02_Supersore/Least_Profitable_Products_Analysis.pdf)

## Project 3 — Northstar Bank: Business Analysis & Transformation Dashboard

**Focus:** Business Analysis | UAT | Requirements Traceability | Defect Management | Service Operations

A two-page simulated banking case study combining requirements traceability, UAT execution, defect monitoring, service-request volumes, resolution time, and SLA classification.

**Dashboard coverage:** UAT Executive Dashboard and Service Request Operations.

**Simulation notice:** Northstar Bank and all associated requirements, test cases, defects, records, and results are fictional and generated for portfolio demonstration. They do not represent real banking operations, customer data, or production UAT.

[Open project files](Project_03_Transform/) · [Power BI report](Project_03_Transform/Transform.pbix) · [PDF](Project_03_Transform/Transform.pdf)

## Overall Learning Objective

These projects demonstrate how data analytics and business intelligence can be applied to different business contexts—from call-centre performance and retail profitability to banking change/testing visibility and service operations. The aim is to translate raw data into structured analysis, meaningful visualisations, and useful business insights.
