# Power BI Portfolio — Business Intelligence & Data Analytics

This repository contains three Power BI projects developed to demonstrate practical skills in data analysis, business intelligence, data visualisation, dashboard development, and business problem-solving.

Each project addresses a different business scenario, beginning with candidate data analysis and progressing through profitability analysis and banking operations performance.

## Projects

### Project 1 — Candidate Intelligence Dashboard

**Focus:** Candidate Data Analysis | Data Cleaning | Data Transformation | Dashboard Development

Analyses candidate-related data to demonstrate how raw information can be validated, cleaned, transformed, and converted into meaningful insights.

**Key areas covered:**
- Data inspection and preparation.
- Data cleaning and validation.
- Candidate analysis and comparison.
- Visualisation of relevant performance indicators.
- Structured analytical reporting.

### Project 2 — Is Least Profitable Always Unprofitable?

**Focus:** Retail Analytics | Profitability Analysis | Geographical Visualisation

Analyses the Sample Superstore dataset using Power BI to investigate product profitability across categories, individual products, geographical markets, and quarterly periods.

**Key dashboard components:**
- Overall Profit by Category.
- Top 10 Least Profitable Items.
- Profit by State.
- Quarterly Profit Trend (2013–2016).
- Department Filter.

**Central business question:**

Are the least profitable products consistently unprofitable, or does their profitability vary across geographical markets?

The project demonstrates why aggregate profitability should not be the only basis for product and portfolio decisions.

### Project 3 — Northstar Bank: Business Analysis & Transformation Dashboard

**Focus:** Business Analysis | UAT | Requirements Traceability | Defect Management | Service Operations

A simulated banking case study combining business analysis artefacts and operational reporting into a two-page Power BI dashboard.

**Page 1 — UAT Executive Dashboard**
- Requirements tracking.
- UAT test execution status.
- Test pass rate.
- Defect severity and status.
- Requirements traceability and test coverage.

**Page 2 — Service Request Operations**
- Total and resolved service requests.
- SLA breach monitoring.
- Average resolution time.
- Service requests by category.
- Resolution time by priority.
- SLA outcomes by assigned team.
- Interactive Category, Priority, and Branch filters.

**Central business question:**

How can a consolidated dashboard help stakeholders identify UAT execution risks and understand where service-request operations may require further investigation?

**Important:** Northstar Bank and all associated data, requirements, test cases, defects, and results are simulated for portfolio demonstration. They do not represent real banking operations or actual production UAT.
